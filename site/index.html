Official Event Info
What the test actually covers

Per the 2027 Division B Rules Manual: up to 2 students, ~50 minutes, may bring 2 Class II calculators and one double-sided 8.5"×11" note sheet. Event Supervisors provide live/preserved specimens, pictures, tables, graphs, and microscopes — expect a lot of images and physical specimen identification, not just a written test.

Topics: plant cell structure & tissues · growth & differentiation · transport of water/gases/nutrients · major plant divisions (algae vs. plants, monocot vs. dicot, gymnosperm vs. angiosperm, woody vs. herbaceous) · history of botany & paleo-botany · plant genetics & reproduction · photosynthesis · role of plants in energy/nutrient cycles · use of plants by animals/humans · competition in plant communities · GMOs · production of foodstuffs · plant diseases (infections & nutrient deficiencies).
