📅 9-Week Study Plan

Today: Sept 28, 2026  →  First Invitational: Saturday, Dec 5, 2026  (≈9.5 weeks)

🤝 Isaac & Alice — Parallel Study

Since Botany is a 2-person event, Isaac and Alice study different topics at the same time each week — not one after the other. That covers all 8 core topics in just 4 weeks instead of 8, leaving 5 weeks for the highest-value part: teaching each other, and practicing with real questions.

Isaac — Structure & Physiology track: cell structure, photosynthesis, vascular anatomy, transport/nutrition

Alice — Diversity & Life Cycle track: algae/land plants, seed plants/paleo-botany, reproduction/GMOs, disease/defense

Both — Weeks 5–10: ecology, teaching each other, and practice tests, together

Week 1 Sep 28 – Oct 4
Foundations — studied in parallel
👦 ISAAC
Plant Cell Structure
  • Cell wall, central vacuole, chloroplast, plasmodesmata — what makes a plant cell different from an animal cell
  • Nucleus, ER, Golgi, mitochondria (quick review)
  • Practice identifying organelles from diagrams and micrographs
📖 Read (26 pp.)
👧 ALICE
Algae & How Plants Colonized Land
  • Green & red algae as the closest relatives of plants — why algae ≠ plants
  • Adaptations for life on land: cuticle, stomata, spores, alternation of generations
  • Bryophytes (mosses) & seedless vascular plants (ferns)
📖 Read (33 pp.)
Week 2 Oct 5 – Oct 11
Studied in parallel
👦 ISAAC
Photosynthesis
  • Chloroplast structure: thylakoid, stroma, grana
  • Light reactions (photosystems I & II, ATP/NADPH) and the Calvin cycle
  • C3 vs. C4 vs. CAM plants — why they evolved, how to tell them apart
📖 Read (25 pp.)

✏️ Practice Test 1
👧 ALICE
Seed Plants & Paleo-botany
  • Gymnosperms vs. angiosperms — "naked" seeds vs. flowers/fruits
  • Monocot vs. dicot — classic ID question (veins, flower parts, roots, stems)
  • Fossil record basics & timeline of plant evolution
📖 Read (26 pp.)
Week 3 Oct 12 – Oct 18
Studied in parallel
👦 ISAAC
Vascular Plant Structure & Growth
  • Roots, stems, leaves — organ-level structure
  • Dermal, vascular, and ground tissue systems
  • Meristems; primary vs. secondary growth
📖 Read (26 pp.)
👧 ALICE
Reproduction, Genetics & GMOs
  • Flower structure & function; pollination methods
  • Angiosperm life cycle — double fertilization, seed & fruit development
  • Plant breeding & genetic engineering — GMO basics
📖 Read (20 pp.)
Week 4 Oct 19 – Oct 25
Studied in parallel — last solo-track week
👦 ISAAC
Transport, Soil & Nutrient Deficiencies
  • Xylem vs. phloem; stomata & transpiration
  • Symptoms of mineral deficiency — likely tested with photos
  • Plant-microbe nutrition (nitrogen-fixing bacteria, mycorrhizae)
📖 Read (38 pp.)
👧 ALICE
Plant Signals, Disease & Defense
  • Plant hormones & tropisms (gravitropism, phototropism)
  • Defenses against pathogens & herbivores — likely tested with photos
  • Fungal plant pathogens & mycorrhizae
📖 Read (43 pp.)
Week 5  Both Together Oct 26 – Nov 1
Ecology: Competition & Nutrient Cycles
Short section — the natural first week to sit down together, now that you've each finished your 4 solo topics.
  • Competition, herbivory, and positive interactions (mutualism, pollination) in plant communities
  • Energy flow through ecosystems; biogeochemical/nutrient cycling
📖 Read (14 pp.)
Week 6  Both Together Nov 2 – Nov 8
Teach Each Other
The payoff for splitting the work: each of you now teaches your teammate your 4 topics, out loud, in your own words. Teaching is one of the strongest ways to find your own gaps — and it gives you both a second pass over all 8 topics.
  • Isaac teaches Alice: cell structure, photosynthesis, vascular anatomy, transport & nutrition
  • Alice teaches Isaac: algae/land plants, seed plants/paleo-botany, reproduction/GMOs, disease/defense
  • Whoever's listening should ask questions and try to stump the other — that's the point
Week 7  Both Together Nov 9 – Nov 15
Practice Tests + Weak-Spot Triage
  • Work through all available practice tests together
  • For anything either of you misses, go back to that week's PDF and reread just that section
✏️ Practice Tests
Week 8  Both Together Nov 16 – Nov 22
Targeted Review + More Practice
  • Revisit weak spots identified in Week 7
  • Retake any practice test questions missed the first time
  • Start building the shared note sheet (double-sided 8.5"×11", allowed in the actual event)
Week 9  Both Together Nov 23 – Nov 29
Full Cumulative Practice Test
  • Take a full-length, timed (~50 min) practice test under real conditions, together
  • Debrief afterward — discuss every question either of you got wrong
Week 10 — Final Push  Both Nov 30 – Dec 4  ·  Competition: Sat, Dec 5
🏆 Light Review Only
  • Finish and pack the note sheet
  • Light review only — skim weak spots, don't cram new material
  • Rest well the night before
